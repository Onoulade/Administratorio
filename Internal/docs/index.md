# Administratorio Documentation

**The factory must grow. Subject to approval.**

## Overview

An overhaul mod for Factorio 2.0 that replaces military conflict with corporate bureaucracy. No guns. No turrets. No military science. Every machine you build, every inserter you place, and every furnace you fire up requires the proper paperwork — and the biters you used to shoot now have HR representation and a grievance process.

## Current State

- **Requires:** Factorio 2.0
- **Status:** Beta — full progression is playable, with ongoing balance, compatibility, translation, and polish work
- **Compatible with:** Space Age, the optional native Quality feature set, Factorissimo, and AAI Loaders
- **Settings:** Working Hours can be disabled for planner mod compatibility

Beta is the public playtesting phase. The core loops, Field Office bootstrap, Space Age planet branches, orbital administration, and optional Quality integration are implemented; balance, compatibility, translation, and late-game polish remain active work. Keep reporting migration issues, awkward balance spikes, missing locale coverage, and places where the paperwork stops being funny and starts being merely paperwork.
